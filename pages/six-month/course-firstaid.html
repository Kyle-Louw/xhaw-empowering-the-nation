First Aid

Comprehensive vocational learnership programme

6 Months Certificate R1,500

Course Overview

Learn essential first aid skills to save lives and respond effectively in emergency situations. This comprehensive 6-month programme covers CPR, emergency scene management, wound care, and more.

What You'll Learn

  • First Aid Awareness & Safety Protocols
  • CPR (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation)
  • Emergency Scene Management
  • Wounds, Bleeding & Bandaging
  • Fractures & Bone Injuries
  • Respiratory Distress Management
  • Recovery Positions & Shock Treatment
  • Choking & Foreign Objects
